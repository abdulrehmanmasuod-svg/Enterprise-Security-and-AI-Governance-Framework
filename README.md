## 📌 Executive Summary
This repository contains a unified suite of enterprise security control plans, risk assessment frameworks, and disaster recovery strategies integrated with modern **AI Governance controls** (aligned with ISO 27001, ISO 42001, and NIST AI RMF).

---

## 📁 Portfolio Documents & Framework Overview

### 1. 🏢 Physical & Environmental Security Controls
* **Document:** `Enterprise Physical & Environmental Security Controls Blueprint`
* **Key Focus:** Access control architecture (biometrics, badge readers), server room physical protection, environmental hazard mitigation (clean agent suppression, UPS), and GPU hardware security for local AI model deployment.

### 2. 📋 Administrative Security & AI Usage Policy
* **Document:** `Organizational Security Awareness & Human Risk Management Policy`
* **Key Focus:** Human risk management, onboarding training programs, quarterly threat communications, and enterprise-wide Acceptable Use Policies (AUP) for Generative AI and Shadow AI prevention.

### 3. 💾 Data Resilience & Disaster Recovery Backup Plan
* **Document:** `Backup_Plan.pdf`
* **Key Focus:** Operationalizing the 3-2-1 backup rule, daily incremental and weekly full backup strategies, and immutable WORM storage protections for proprietary AI model weights and training datasets.

### 4. ⚠️ Corporate Cyber Risk & AI Tolerability Assessment
* **Document:** `Corporate Cyber Risk & Vulnerability Tolerability Assessment`
* **Key Focus:** Threat modeling across internal/external vectors, risk tolerance scoring, CVSS/Harm impact mapping, and risk controls for Prompt Injection and third-party AI vendor vulnerabilities.
